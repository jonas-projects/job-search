    How to fill this in

    • Skip anything that doesn't apply. Nothing is required, and it's fine to do this over several sittings.
    • Rough numbers beat no numbers. "About 40 tickets a week", "cut turnaround roughly in half" or "~$20k saved" are all useful. Mark guesses with "~".
    • Write the way you'd explain it to a colleague. Notes and fragments are fine; Claude turns them into CV wording.
    • NDAs: if work is confidential, describe it generically ("a large retail client") and say in the NDA box what can be named.
    • Nothing here goes on a CV without you approving the wording first.
